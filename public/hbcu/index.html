The Org Square

One square.
Reserved for the org.

On every HBCU initiative board, the first square is reserved for the participating organization — marked with their initials, randomly assigned numbers just like every other square. If that square wins a period, those winnings go directly to the org.

The Donation

Your winnings
stay yours.

A portion of platform fees from every HBCU initiative board are donated to the participating organization. Your winnings are never touched — every dollar you win is yours to keep.

💰
Platform fees fund the mission
Daali donates a portion of platform fees from every board in this initiative directly to the participating HBCU alumni organization.
🏆
Winners still win everything
Participant winnings are completely separate. The donation never comes out of what the board pays out — what you win, you keep.
🎯
Two ways the org benefits
Every board contributes a platform fee donation, and if the org's reserved square wins a period, those winnings go to them too.
