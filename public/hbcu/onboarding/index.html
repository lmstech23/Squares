After You Sign Up

What happens next

1
Your organization is listed on the fundraiser page
Your chapter name and logo will appear on the Daali HBCU initiative page, visible to participants looking for a board to join.
2
Daali creates a board for your community
We handle the full setup — game selection, pricing, payout structure. No logistics on your end.
3
Share the link with your alumni network
Once the board is live, you'll receive an email with a shareable link. Drop it in your group chat, newsletter, or social channels — and let your network play.
Transparent Fundraising

How the donation works

🔒
What your organization can count on
✓
A portion of Daali platform fees from your board go to your scholarship fund. The donation comes from platform fees — separate from what participants win.
✓
Participant winnings are never touched. Every dollar a participant wins goes directly to them. The donation and the winnings are completely independent.
✓
Your org has a square in every board. One square on every board is reserved with your initials. If it wins a period, those winnings go to your organization too.
✓
Boards are fully managed through the Daali platform. Your organization doesn't run anything — you just receive the donation and share the link.
