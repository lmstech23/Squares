Two ways to use Daali Boards

Getting started

1
Sign in and hit "New Board"
From your host dashboard, tap New Board. If this is your first time, you'll be asked how you want to collect payments — card (via Stripe) or cash only. You can always add Stripe later.
2
Fill in the game details
Enter a game name, team names (one per axis), and the price per square (minimum $1). These are the only required fields — the rest have smart defaults.
game name team A · team B $ per square
3
Set your payout structure
Choose halves or quarters and split the pot per period. You can also set a host cut (0–50%, default 20%). The payout percentages must add up to 100%.
Tip: Even splits work great for casual pools. The system validates totals automatically so there are no math surprises.
4
Add your payout info (optional but recommended)
Add your Venmo, CashApp, Zelle, or PayPal handle so winners know exactly how to get paid. You control whether this is visible to everyone or only after a PIN. If cash mode is on, at least one handle is required.
5
Your board is live — share the link
After creation, you land on your board dashboard with a copy link button front and center. Drop the link in your group chat. Players don't need accounts — they just tap the link and pick a square.
Numbers randomize when you close the board — so every square has equal odds until then. No one can game position early.

1
Track your board in real time
Your dashboard shows a live fill tracker (X / 100 squares), pending payment count, and the full 10×10 grid color-coded by status. You always know exactly where things stand.
2
Enable Cash Mode (optional)
Toggle Cash Reservations on to generate a 4-digit PIN. Share the PIN with players paying in person — they enter it to reserve a square, then hand you the cash. You confirm receipt on your dashboard to lock their square green.
Cash TTL: Unconfirmed cash reservations expire automatically after 20 minutes, so squares don't stay locked indefinitely.
3
Reserve squares for cash players
From the Cash Reserve panel, you can reserve a square by player name on their behalf, then confirm cash received to lock it — or release it back to open if they didn't pay.
4
Close the board when you're ready
Hit Close Board and confirm. The system instantly shuffles and assigns random 0–9 numbers to rows and columns (Fisher-Yates). If you have unconfirmed cash squares, you'll be prompted to confirm, release, or cancel before closing.
5
Enter scores and see winners instantly
After the board is closed, enter scores per period for each team. Winners are calculated automatically based on the last digit of each score matching the row/column numbers.
6
Winner cards tell you exactly how to pay
Each period generates a winner card with everything you need in one glance: the player's name, score match, dollar amount, and their preferred payout method.

No app. No sign-up. Just a link.

1
Tap the link the host shared
Players open the board link directly — no account needed, no app download. They see the game name, team names, square price, and the live 10×10 grid showing which squares are open or taken.
2
Pick an open square
Tap any empty square. A checkout form appears. What they see depends on how the host set up payments:
Card only Cash only Card + Cash tabs
3
Pay by card (Stripe)
Enter name, email, and phone, then tap Pay. They're redirected to a Stripe checkout page. On success, the square locks green immediately and they see a confirmation banner.
Pending timeout: If a player starts checkout but doesn't complete it, the square stays yellow (pending) and releases automatically — no manual cleanup needed.
4
Pay by cash (PIN required)
Enter name and the 4-digit PIN the host shared. The square turns amber (reserved). Then hand the cash to the host — they confirm on their dashboard and it goes green.
Heads up: The square stays reserved for 20 minutes. If the host hasn't confirmed by then, it releases back to open.
5
Watch the board fill up
Players can return to the board link any time to see fill progress. Once the host closes the board, row and column numbers appear. Winners are highlighted per period with payout amounts shown.
6
Add payout info to get paid faster (optional)
If the host asks players to share payout info, there's a simple field to enter a Venmo, CashApp, Zelle, or PayPal handle. This lets the host pay winners in one tap — no group chat DMs needed.
